# Prompts para ChatGPT (DALL-E) — Estaca, Cuerda y Cortina

No tengo capacidad de generar imágenes en este entorno. El PPTX ya tiene 5 espacios marcados (paneles oscuros con el logo de fondo y una etiqueta) donde podés insertar estas imágenes una vez generadas. Todas están escritas para mantener el mismo estilo de marca (ilustración plana, sin fotorrealismo, paleta ink/cream/green de El Renuevo) para que las 5 se vean como un mismo set.

Pegá cada prompt tal cual en ChatGPT (con generación de imágenes DALL-E activada).

---

## 1. Introducción — Carpa bien armada

```
Flat minimal vector illustration, wide landscape aspect ratio (about 2:1).
A well-pitched camping tent at night: taut guy-lines, stakes firmly driven
into the ground, dome shape standing strong and steady against the wind.
Style: clean flat illustration with bold simple shapes, no gradients, no
photorealism, no shading detail — editorial poster style. Color palette:
background deep charcoal black (#1A1A1A), tent and rope accents in a single
vivid lime green (#5DBE2C), highlights in warm cream (#F7F1E8). Minimal
composition, lots of negative space, no text, no logos, no watermarks.
```

## 2. Introducción — Carpa mal armada

```
Flat minimal vector illustration, wide landscape aspect ratio (about 2:1),
companion piece to a "well-pitched tent" illustration in the exact same
style and palette. A poorly pitched camping tent during a rainstorm at
night: sagging fabric, loose slack ropes, a crooked stake half pulled out
of the ground, the tent leaning and collapsing under wind and rain. Style:
same flat, bold, minimal illustration language — no gradients, no
photorealism, editorial poster style. Color palette: deep charcoal black
background (#1A1A1A), tent and rope in lime green (#5DBE2C) with muted
gray-green for rain/wind streaks, cream (#F7F1E8) highlights. Minimal,
lots of negative space, no text, no logos, no watermarks.
```

## 3. Punto 1 — La Estaca

```
Flat minimal vector illustration, portrait aspect ratio (about 3:4).
Close-up of a single tent stake (peg) driven firmly into the ground, with
an angled guy-line rope attached and pulling taut from it. Style: bold
flat shapes, no gradients, no photorealism, editorial poster look. Color
palette: charcoal black background (#1A1A1A), stake and rope rendered in
vivid lime green (#5DBE2C), small warm cream (#F7F1E8) highlight accents.
Minimal composition, plenty of negative space, no text, no logos.
```

## 4. Punto 2 — La Cuerda

```
Flat minimal vector illustration, portrait aspect ratio (about 3:4). A
single taut tent guy-line rope stretching diagonally across the frame,
anchored to a stake at one end and to a tent's canvas at the other end,
visibly showing tension and connection between the two. Style: bold flat
shapes, no gradients, no photorealism, editorial poster look. Color
palette: charcoal black background (#1A1A1A), rope and stake in vivid
lime green (#5DBE2C), warm cream (#F7F1E8) highlight accents. Minimal
composition, plenty of negative space, no text, no logos.
```

## 5. Punto 3 — La Cortina / Tienda

```
Flat minimal vector illustration, portrait aspect ratio (about 3:4). View
from inside a tent looking at the glowing canvas walls at night, warm
light diffusing through the fabric, a cozy inhabited feel suggesting
shelter and community. Style: bold flat shapes, no gradients, no
photorealism, editorial poster look. Color palette: charcoal black
(#1A1A1A) exterior tones, canvas glowing in warm cream (#F7F1E8) with
lime green (#5DBE2C) light accents. Minimal, atmospheric, plenty of
negative space, no text, no logos.
```

---

**Cómo insertarlas después:** una vez generadas, decime y las coloco yo mismo en los 5 espacios marcados del PPTX (o lo hacés directamente en PowerPoint: Insertar → Imágenes, sobre cada panel oscuro con la etiqueta correspondiente).
