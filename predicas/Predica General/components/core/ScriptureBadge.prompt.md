Pill chip carrying a Bible reference. Sits under a quote or beside a title.

```jsx
<ScriptureBadge>2 Corintios 5:17</ScriptureBadge>
<ScriptureBadge variant="outline">Juan 3:16</ScriptureBadge>
```

- `solid` for cream slides and dark boxes; `outline` reads best on ink.
- Reference only — book, chapter, verse. Not for full quotes (use Playfair italic for those).
